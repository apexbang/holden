# Ledger

Durable decisions that constrain future work. One entry each: what was decided, why, where the
evidence is, when, and optionally what would reopen it. Promoted here from a phase's DECISION.md by /h-grill, or written by
/h-milestone close. Never edit an entry; add a new one that supersedes it and say so.

## L001 (2026-01-01) Example decision
Decided: one sentence. Because: one sentence. Evidence: path or commit. Supersedes: none.
Revisit when: the observable condition that would reopen it (a count, a version, a second
tenant), or leave the field out.
