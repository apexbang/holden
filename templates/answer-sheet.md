# Answer sheet

One block per decision, for every decision on the list, in the list's order:

## Dxx <the decision's title, as the list words it>
Pick: <your choice>  Confidence: high | medium | low
Why: <at most two sentences, each citing a path:line or an F-id from the list's facts>

For a decision you would leave to the operator, the block holds one line in place of Pick and Why:

## Dxx <the decision's title>
Not my call: <why, in a clause>

Write nothing before the first block or after the last.
